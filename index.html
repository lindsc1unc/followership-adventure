Chapter 13 • Followership

Choose Your Own Followership Adventure

Your table is part of the ambulatory operations team at a large health care organization. You will make four decisions under increasing pressure. Each choice changes what happens next—and a choice can be defensible while still creating real costs.

Rules: Discuss each decision as a table and use one device. Aim for about 2 minutes per decision. Once a choice is locked in, you cannot go back. There is no guaranteed “correct” route: choose what your group would genuinely do in a real organization, not what you think the presenters want.
